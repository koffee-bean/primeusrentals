Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Do you allow pets?

Most homes, yes, with a pet deposit and a short pet profile. Each listing says what's allowed.

How long are leases?

Twelve months is standard. Some homes offer month-to-month after the first year.

What's the application process?

Online application, credit and background check, and income verification. Every applicant is screened the same way, and we respond within two business days.

Who do I call when something breaks?

Us. There's no third-party property manager. Submit a request online or email support@primeusrentals.com and you'll hear back within 24 hours.
