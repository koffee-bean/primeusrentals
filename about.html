About

Managed directly, start to finish.

Prime US Rentals manages a small portfolio of single-family homes across Washington, Idaho and Nevada. We keep it small on purpose, so every home gets the attention it should.

How we run things

The short version

No call center, no ticket queue. When you email support@primeusrentals.com you reach the team that manages the home, and we'd rather fix something properly once than cheaply three times.

We keep leases readable, deposits itemized, and move-out inspections done together so there are no surprises. Rent is paid online through a resident portal, and maintenance requests go through the same place so nothing gets lost in a text thread.

We've been tenants too. That shapes most of the decisions we make.
